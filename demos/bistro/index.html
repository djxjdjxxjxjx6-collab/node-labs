Our story

A small kitchen with big intentions.

Saffron & Oak began as a weekend supper club in a tiny flat. Today it's a twelve-table room where we cook the food we love — honest, seasonal, and made from scratch every day.

We work with local growers, cure and bake in-house, and keep the menu small so everything that leaves the pass is something we're proud of.

— Elena & Marco, founders
